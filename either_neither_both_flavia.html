Grammar Practice

Either, Neither, Both
& Whether

Four words B1→B2 students often confuse — practised through your world as a geologist.

Level B1→B2 Geology & Fieldwork 5 exercises + speaking

either … or

one OR the other (not both)

Either the GPS or the compass will help us navigate.
→ one of two options
You can use either method — both work.
→ any one of the two (free choice)
Either of the samples is suitable.
→ either + of + plural noun, singular verb

neither … nor

not one AND not the other

Neither the drill nor the corer was working.
→ negative for both
Neither of the results was reliable.
→ neither + of + plural noun, singular verb
"Basalt or granite?" — "Neither."
→ standing alone as a negative answer

both … and

the two together, at the same time

Both travertines and limestone are carbonates.
→ includes two, always plural
Both methods gave consistent results.
→ both + plural noun
She is both a geologist and a volcanologist.
→ two roles at once

whether

if — indirect questions & alternatives

We need to check whether the equipment is working.
→ indirect yes/no question
I'm not sure whether to use method A or B.
→ deciding between options
Whether or not it rains, we continue.
→ regardless of one alternative

Exercise 3 — Error correction

  1. Erro: "or" → neither … nor
  2. Erro: "if whether" → usar apenas "whether" (nunca os dois juntos)
  3. Erro: "was" → were (both … and → plural verb)
  4. ✅ CORRETA — "either of" = qualquer um dos dois é aceitável. Bom ponto para discutir: either = escolha livre; both = os dois juntos.

Item 4 é uma armadilha proposital. Vale discutir por que está correto.
